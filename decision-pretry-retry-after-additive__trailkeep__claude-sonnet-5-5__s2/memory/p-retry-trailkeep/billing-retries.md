## Decisions and dead ends

- 2026-11-06: Retry-After work is on hold: the user wants to look at their 429 dashboards first, so don't start it until they say so. Decided by the user: wait the Retry-After time PLUS the normal backoff delay (not instead of it, not the max of the two). Reason: billing's Retry-After is the moment their rate window reopens, and ~40 pods returning at that exact moment get 429'd again; the backoff on top spreads them out. Client already sets error.retryAfter in seconds. Not yet decided: the API shape (my suggestion was an option that returns a delay in ms, called where calculateDelay is, so retryDelay in the callbacks includes it), whether maxTimeout caps the Retry-After part, what happens when it doesn't fit in maxRetryTime (today the wait is shortened with Math.min), and whether randomize applies to it. Ask the user about those before building.
- 2026-11-06: The user lifted the hold on Retry-After work (said 'build it now'), but then stopped for the day before answering the open design questions, so nothing is built yet. Next session: ask them to confirm (or say 'go with your recommendations') on these four points. They are MY recommendations, not user decisions: (1) API: a retryAfter(error) => ms option called where calculateDelay is (index.js ~183) so retryDelay includes it; alternative is auto-reading numeric error.retryAfter by default, which the user's wording hinted at; (2) maxTimeout caps only the backoff part, not Retry-After, because Retry-After is the server's reopen time; (3) if Retry-After plus backoff doesn't fit maxRetryTime, stop and throw rather than shorten with Math.min, because retrying before the window reopens wastes an attempt; (4) randomize applies to the backoff part only.
